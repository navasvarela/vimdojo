Privacy

vimdojo is a personal open-source project, run by @navasvarela. The game needs no account and works offline. This page covers what is stored if you visit the site, or choose to create an account.

Playing without an account

The plugin keeps your progress and your best key logs on your own machine, and doesn't send them anywhere. The demo keeps its progress in your browser. Neither sets a cookie.

The site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and doesn't track you across sites.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional, and you sign in with GitHub. vimdojo asks GitHub for no scopes.

WhatWhyHow long
Your GitHub numeric id To recognise you when you log in again Until you delete your account
The handle you pick Your public name on the leaderboards Until you delete your account. A released handle stays reserved for 90 days, unlinked from you, so nobody can impersonate you.
Your logins: the kind, and when each was created and last used. Only a hash of each token. To keep you signed in, and to let you revoke a login 30 days after last use, or until you sign out
A hash of your IP address, with a salt that changes daily To limit abuse of the login routes About a day. The address itself isn't stored.

vimdojo doesn't collect your email, name or avatar, and discards the rest of what GitHub returns at login. There are no ads and no selling of data. Your handle is public.

One cookie is used, and only if you sign in on this site: a session cookie that is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. A short-lived second cookie protects the login itself.

Your data, your call

On your account page you can see your logins and revoke them, download everything stored about you as JSON, and delete your account. Deleting removes your data at once. Questions, or anything else you'd like to ask: open an issue.

Who handles the data

  • Cloudflare runs the API and its database, which is kept in the EU, and provides the visit counts.
  • GitHub provides sign-in, and hosts this site.
